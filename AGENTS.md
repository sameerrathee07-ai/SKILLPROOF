# SkillProof Project Rules

## Design & Aesthetic Principles
- **No Purple Gradients:** Never use purple, violet, or indigo gradients. Use grounded slates, deep neutrals, or clear solid accents.
- **No Pill-Shaped Buttons:** Avoid `rounded-full` buttons, pill tabs, or pill badges. Use clean `rounded-md` or `rounded-lg`.
- **No Vague Hero Texts:** Copy must be concrete, truthful, and domain-specific.
- **No AI-Slop Photos or Copy:** No synthetic stock graphics, no hollow marketing fluff.
- **No Emoji Icons:** Use real SVG icons (Lucide), never emoji in UI chrome or headings.
- **No Em Dashes (`—`):** Use colons (`:`) or standard hyphens (`-`).
- **No Cursor Animations or Heavy Scroll Animations:** Keep interactions responsive, snappy, and native.
- **No Fake Reviews or Metrics:** Zero fabricated social proof, testimonials, or fake counters.

## Launch Criteria
1. Custom domain connected and configured (`docs/DOMAIN_SETUP.md`).
2. Favicon live (`app/icon.svg`).
3. Zero "Made with AI" labels or badges anywhere.
4. Privacy Policy page live (`app/privacy/page.tsx`).
5. Terms and Conditions page live (`app/terms/page.tsx`).
6. Zero defects.
